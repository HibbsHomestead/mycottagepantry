# Subscription proposal — generous Mixing Bowl and optional Free

This proposal supersedes the earlier Essentials/Business/Team split and all prior small paid recipe/product/inventory limits. It changes the public pricing preview and central design configuration only. No billing provider, subscription assignment, restriction, archival action, data migration, inventory movement or financial entry was activated. Existing workspace access and data remain unchanged.

Preview: `/pricing`. Public signup, beta collection and checkout remain closed. The preferred launch offer is Free plus optional upgrades, without a simultaneous 14-day trial. No perpetual-free or unlimited-resource commitment is made. AI and shopping comparison remain deferred, independently disabled by default on the server; neither is an entitlement or upgrade benefit.

Revised draft prices (Free $0, Mixing Bowl $10, Baker’s Bench $20, Cottage Kitchen $30, Cottage Mercantile $40) supersede the earlier $25/$40 and $50 proposals. No annual price is set.

Mixing Bowl includes ingredients, packaging, recipes, products, complete costing and verified conversions; labor, overhead, packaging and waste inputs; deterministic text/link recipe import with editable review; recipe/product links and pricing scenarios; receiving, adjustments, balances, history, low-stock status and reorder thresholds; individual batch planning/completion; label design/print/PDF with logos, fonts and saved designs; sales, expenses, basic profitability and existing business/tax reports; exports and essential account mail. No small commercial record counts are proposed for paid plans. File-size, request, abuse and resource safeguards still need review; absence of a commercial count cap is not a promise of infinite resources.

Every plan includes essential warnings, privacy/security controls and export of data the member is authorized to access, including historical data created on an earlier plan. Verification, recovery, security/account-change notices and applicable subscription notices belong on every plan. They are not premium perks. Actual essential-mail delivery still needs verification; see [email audit](email-scope.md). No optional reminder emails are advertised as available.

## Active versus archived — proposed, not implemented

- An **active recipe** is a current editable recipe available for new product links or batch scheduling. Every recipe/prep ID counts once, regardless of how many products reference it. An **active product** is available for new business operations; variants with separate IDs count separately. Only explicit server-owned `archived_at` state excludes a record; filtering/search visibility does not.
- An **active tracked stock item** is an unarchived ingredient/packaging/prep record with tracking explicitly enabled. Cost records defaulting to cost-only would not count. Current legacy defaults need reviewed backfill, not reinterpretation based on browser values.
- **Archived** means explicitly retired from new selections, not deleted or inaccessible. Keep stable IDs, historical snapshots, ledger references, recipe/product links, original source and readable/exportable detail. Current links remain intact. Previously completed batches and posted journals remain immutable.
- Do not archive a recipe/product needed by a pending batch or active dependent operation. Warn with dependency lists and require the user to finish/cancel/relink those future operations explicitly. Existing active product links must remain functional: block archiving the linked recipe while those links require it rather than orphan it or duplicate it.
- Do not archive/disable tracking for stock with nonzero balance or pending commitments. Require explicit, ledger-backed resolution first; never erase balances to free a slot. Historical zero-stock items may be archived without rewriting history.
- Reactivation consumes a slot and follows the same transactional check as creation/enabling tracking. Renames, quantity/price corrections and ordinary edits do not create extra records or get blocked solely because the count is at its cap. Corrections still obey normal authorization and immutable-record rules. A correction must not conceal creation/reactivation of additional active records.

No archival workflow is added in this task. Actual schema/state and dependency rules require review before any migration or limit is enabled.

## Stock coverage before batch completion

Proposed backend preview lists **every** required recipe input and applicable output packaging, required/actual quantity, verified conversion, tracking state, available stock and any shortage. Distinguish cost coverage from inventory coverage: a cost-only ingredient can cost a recipe without establishing an on-hand balance.

Completion in Free’s tracked-stock mode requires all required inputs to be tracked, convertible and sufficiently stocked. If any input is cost-only, unknown, missing or beyond the available tracking slots, block completion with a clear explanation and preserve the draft; do not silently deduct only the tracked subset. Let the user review/enable tracking within the allowance or revise the plan explicitly. An alternative non-inventory batch mode needs separate review before implementation; no automatic fallthrough is proposed.

Recheck coverage and stock in the same transaction as completion. An explicit packaging choice cannot bypass required packaging. Optional recipe additions not selected for this batch must be clearly excluded, rather than treated as used. No inventory mutation occurs during coverage preview or pricing evaluation.

## Server-side limits and concurrency — future implementation design

After owner review, use Netlify Database for subscription/version, active state, membership and any count/provisioning records. Derive workspace from verified membership, never client input. In a transaction, lock a workspace-level capacity/subscription row **before** counting or changing active records. All creation, duplication, import approval, bulk import, product-link creation, reactivation, tracking enablement and future background jobs must acquire the same lock and recheck subscription version/counts. Enforce a consistent lock order and idempotency keys for retries.

At 4 active recipes, two concurrent creates must yield one success and one clear capacity response, never 6 active rows. Validate bulk operations as a whole; no unexpected partial import or automatic Pantry creation. Do not count an unapproved import draft as an active recipe; approving it consumes capacity transactionally. An edit of an existing active record at 5 remains possible. Server limit tests must include concurrent creates/reactivation, stale clients, downgraded over-cap data and imports, plus tenant boundaries.

Role, membership, subscription lifecycle, feature grant and usage/resource safeguards remain separate. The centralized configuration and prepared evaluator are **not imported into the workspace API**. Changing preview data alone must never change real access.

## Downgrade, cancellation and retention — proposal for review

No existing customer subscription, role, record or access changes automatically.

1. Preview the target plan, counts, member seats, dependent pending work and changed capabilities before confirmation. Schedule a paid downgrade for the end of its paid period; final dates, cancellation/grace/refund terms require owner/legal review.
2. Preserve all records, files, source, links, balances, historical snapshots and audit history. Never auto-delete, auto-archive, truncate a recipe, rewrite a sale or deduct stock to fit a plan.
3. If over Free’s active counts, grandfather existing active records for reading, export, normal corrections and safe completion of already planned work. Block **net new** creation/reactivation/tracking increases until the user explicitly resolves counts or upgrades. Server logic must allow ordinary corrections while preventing count increases. Explain coverage for every batch; never partially consume to satisfy a cap.
4. Preserve access to historical sales/expenses/reports and export. Free’s proposed exclusion concerns new financial workflows, not hiding past data. Corrections to posted financial records require a controlled reversal/correction path; if a downgrade would prevent essential reconciliation, defer that portion until a safe path exists. Do not lock users out of fixing their books.
5. For a five-to-one-seat downgrade, require explicit Owner-selected membership resolution and backend revocation; no silent demotion/removal and never remove the last Owner. Until reviewed resolution exists, do not apply that downgrade automatically.
6. Prefer Free after an explicitly approved cancellation path, with the over-limit grandfathering above. Failed payments should use the provider’s native notice/retry process and a reviewed grace period before restricting new activity. No grace duration, trial expiry or access migration is enabled here. There is no launch trial.
7. Proposed retention: retain archived and over-limit records while the workspace remains open, with export and member-authorized access. Do not introduce a silent inactivity purge for Free. Before promising indefinite retention, approve storage budgets, inactive-account contact, legal record obligations, account-versus-workspace deletion and a notice/export window. No exact deletion period or legal policy is invented.

## Operating-cost assumptions — capacity model, not an account quote

Free has no payment processing and no credit card, but still incurs authentication, compute, data, mail and support costs. Existing provider contracts and actual Netlify/Identity quotas have **not** been verified. The following workload and budgeting assumptions are deliberately explicit; they are not measured unit prices or guarantees of included free service.

| Input per monthly active Free workspace | Planning assumption                                                                   | 100 active workspaces | 1,000 active workspaces |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------: | ----------------------: |
| Identities                              | 1 current user                                                                        |                   100 |                   1,000 |
| Authenticated API requests              | 1,000/month, no page-load provider searches                                           |               100,000 |               1,000,000 |
| Database footprint                      | 10 MB including catalog, drafts, ledger/history and index allowance                   |                  1 GB |                   10 GB |
| Private files                           | 20 MB retained average, with future upload/retention safeguards                       |                  2 GB |                   20 GB |

| Support                                 | 5% contact monthly; 10 minutes/contact; $30/hour planning labor cost                  |      50 minutes / $25 |      500 minutes / $250 |

File and history storage accumulate even when a workspace is inactive; monthly-active counts alone understate retention cost. Database queries/transactions, indexes, functions, bandwidth, Blob operations/egress, backups, session refresh and abusive signup attempts must be measured. The existing anonymous demo provisioning path is an abuse/cost launch blocker, not the proposed Free signup path. Essential email access is never a paid upgrade; abuse controls apply equally.

## Separate account actions from code

**Owner/provider accounts:** review Free scope and downgrade/retention rules; verify actual hosting/Database/Blob/Identity quotas and costs; approve support/legal content; verify production domain/TLS and recovery/verification links; inspect authenticated mail sender/reply/limits; approve any eventual delivery test. Consider a hosted billing provider only after launch gates and explicit activation approval. External AI/shopping subscriptions, if active, need separate owner cancellation; hiding features cannot cancel billing.

**Code work after review:** empty idempotent onboarding, real tenant/session/permission proof, active/archive lifecycle and transactional limits, explicit stock coverage, safe downgraded access/corrections, standard-label Free scope, complete export/restore/retention controls, essential mail UX and abuse protection. Then implement advanced planning/period comparisons; later invitations/seat administration. No automatic migrations, public signup, checkout or live payment products are authorized by this proposal.

See [public release status](public-release.md), [account audit](accounts-and-launch-audit.md) and [email scope](email-scope.md). Readiness is determined by behavior and evidence, never a successful build alone.
