## Square proposal — not implemented

The existing `squareProvider.fetchSales` throws immediately. There is no Square OAuth route, callback, token storage, API client, webhook, import staging or product mapping. Its accurate classification is **UI placeholder only**, not fully implemented but unconfigured. Both existing UI surfaces now say **Planned** or **Not yet available** and offer no Connect action.

Proposed first implementation:

1. An authenticated Owner manually selects Square merchant locations, one currency and explicit inclusive calendar start/end dates in each location's timezone. Convert these into start-inclusive/end-exclusive provider timestamps. No background schedule or implicit historical backfill. Clearly show the covered dates and locations before fetching.
2. Read completed orders and their payment/refund/fee details into private workspace staging. Preserve source IDs, timestamps, currency and original amounts. Match Square catalog variation IDs to workspace product IDs using user-approved mappings. Names and SKUs can suggest a match but cannot establish one. Unmatched lines remain unresolved; never silently create products.
3. Preserve gross sales, line and order discounts, net sales and collected tax separately. Use the provider's line allocation of discounts and taxes; unresolved allocation requires review. Do not infer tax rates or recalculate historical provider tax using current workspace rates. Keep processing fees outside sales and tax, link them to the payment, and mark unavailable/finalization-pending fees explicitly. Show tips, gift cards, service charges and other non-product amounts separately as unresolved until an approved accounting mapping exists. Do not treat payout net deposits as revenue.
4. Link partial/full refunds to original order, payment and line IDs, preserving original sales. Import provider tax reversals and fee adjustments separately; never assume fees are refunded or goods are restocked. Unallocated refunds stay in review.
5. Enforce durable uniqueness for workspace + merchant/location + provider order, payment and refund IDs. Overlapping date ranges must not duplicate records. Refetches may update unapproved staging; source changes after approval require an explicit correction, preserving history. Reference source IDs in any later approved posting to prevent duplicate posting.
6. Importing and reviewing have no stock or financial posting effects. Any future stock adjustment requires a separately confirmed product/quantity mapping, accounting for previously recorded manual sales or production, and a stock ledger record. Refund restocking requires a separate choice. No automatic inventory deduction or posting is included in this audit or the initial staging scope.
7. Present a review showing matched/unmatched lines, discounts, taxes, fees, refunds, duplicates and excluded/non-product amounts. Reconcile orders against captured payments, refunds and settlements, with timing differences visible. User review and reconciliation precede any separately designed posting workflow. Keep manual entries visible to flag possible double counting.

## Tax and AI boundaries

TaxCenter, reportRows and CSV downloads implement reports, not filing or submission. The Integrations page links to working reports and says no filing service exists.

AI is independent of shopping. `AI_ENABLED` requires exact server-side `true`; unset defaults OFF. `/api/workspace?assistant=1` is rejected before database/auth/provider access. Handler and provider adapter also guard inference. `receiptExtract` is rejected before creating attempts/reserving usage or changing draft values; the receipt provider and recipe document provider each check the flag before constructing a client. No automatic receipt extraction occurs after upload. Receipt attachments and manual review remain. Recipe text, text files, CookBook text, secure URL extraction, instructions review and product-link import do not require AI. Uploaded recipe PDFs/images preserve an original and manual-review draft with an honest unavailable message, never an automatic paid fallback. Instructions cannot silently become ingredient rows.

Assistant buttons, drawers, ingredient-context actions and the AI settings panel are unmounted. No public coming-soon placeholder or AI plan benefit was added. Provider code/tests remain internal. Future re-enable needs product approval, explicit usage pricing/limits, provider and deployment-bound status checks, reviewed UI restoration, per-operation authorization after waits, and deliberate owner-approved requests. Do not activate by adding credentials alone. Retained assistant limits are 15-second cooldown; 10/hour and 30/day per user; 30/hour and 100/day per workspace. OCR needs its own approved usage budget before future release; do not treat assistant limits as OCR limits.

The earlier 401 HTML probes from site protection do not prove missing configuration or provider failure. No live AI request was made. Essential security/account email and manual deterministic operations are independent of AI.
