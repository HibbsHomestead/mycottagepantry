# Business registration and subscription signup: audit and release proposal

This document records the first-stage audit before implementation. See [the private implementation status](private-registration-status.md) for the subsequent approvals, code changes and remaining blockers.

This is the first delivery requested before implementation. It audits the current source and read-only provider responses, proposes the signup screens and storage model, and identifies approval and account-configuration dependencies. It does not enable registration, modify authentication settings, create Square objects, grant complimentary access, migrate customer records, or authorize charges. The current request establishes monthly USD prices; older documents describing currency as undecided are superseded on that point. Existing draft entitlements remain the source of truth until reviewed. Historical audits are background, not evidence that the current implementation matches them.

## 2. Proposed screen flow

Use the public domain `https://mycottagepantry.com` as trusted production configuration only after its callbacks/TLS are verified. Allow exact test origins separately; never use an arbitrary Host header or client redirect URL for auth/payment destinations.

1. **Plan selection — `/pricing`.** Four cards use the central plan registry. While closed, each shows **Join the beta**, preserves only a validated stable plan ID, and explains preview availability. Baker’s Bench and Cottage Kitchen remain preview/contact mode until their additions pass acceptance. AI and shopping comparison are absent. A URL plan value is a preference, never a price or access grant.
2. **Create account — `/signup`.** First enforce the approved beta eligibility mechanism; there must be no direct callable public signup bypass. Show a compact progress indicator, selected plan, full name, email and provider-managed password with password-manager autocomplete/show-hide. Required unchecked acceptance links to the actual reviewed Terms and Privacy versions. Optional marketing checkbox is separate and initially unchecked. Legal content not reviewed means no production acceptance form. Include **Already have an account? Log in.** No payment details.
3. **Verify email — `/verify-email`, callback handled through `/login`.** Say that a confirmation was requested, not delivered. Show the intended plan, change-address/retry/help actions and rate-limited resend. Invalid, expired or consumed links offer sign-in or a fresh supported link without revealing account existence. Same-browser safe draft uses an opaque, HttpOnly intent handle; after auth, associate the intent transactionally with the verified identity. For a different browser with no handle, recover only provider-verified/server-associated intent; otherwise ask the user to reselect a plan. Never guess from email text or URL tokens. No business is activated while unverified.
4. **Invitation branch.** Resolve a valid app workspace invitation before starting business creation. Identity invitations and workspace membership invitations are different objects. A verified recipient accepts the existing workspace/role transactionally under a seat lock; no new business. Revoked, expired, mismatched or unsupported membership invites fail explicitly. Until this is implemented and tested, do not offer team signup or treat an auth invite as workspace access.
5. **Business setup — `/onboarding`.** Heading **“Let’s make room for your business.”** Supporting text **“Tell us a little about your business. You can update these details later.”** Required business/display name, country/region, IANA timezone, visible USD reporting currency, and US customary/metric display choice. Browser-suggested timezone and region must be visible/editable. USD limitation is explicit. Optional phone, website/social URL, description, safe logo upload and baked goods/other cottage foods/handmade/mixed focus. No tax ID, government ID, permit, license or home address. Explain that this creates an app workspace, not a legal business or certification, and that home addresses are not publicly displayed by default. Focus does not grant features; explain that specialized handmade production is unavailable.
6. **Activate or review checkout.** Verified setup creates one empty workspace and Owner membership with safe initial state. Free requires no card, trial or Square object; once approved Free scope is ready, show **“You’re ready to get started.”** For eligible paid plans, show server-resolved monthly USD price, approved tax calculation, exact due-today amount, start/renewal dates and cancellation terms. Consent is unchecked and versioned. If total/tax is unresolved, disable payment; do not show zero tax as a placeholder. A final payment control must clearly state the actual amount/action (for example, **Pay $10.00 and subscribe monthly**, only when that is the confirmed total). Preview plans have no payment control.
7. **Payment status — `/onboarding/payment`.** Returning from checkout shows **“We’re checking your payment.”** Backend status refresh/reconciliation cannot start another charge. Lost tab, timeout and ambiguous response reuse the unresolved attempt. Retain workspace/account on failure. Explicit Free selection must first resolve/cancel the pending paid workflow and confirm it cannot bill later; never silently abandon it.
8. **Workspace welcome.** Own empty records, own settings and Cottage Purple. A skippable, resumable checklist covers first ingredient/material where supported, package quantity/price, recipe/import where entitled, product link, costing, and supported inventory/batch flow. Basic product/sales flexibility does not imply handmade-material production. Cottage Kitchen invites appear only after activation and tested collaboration exists. Demo is a separate labeled experience, never a live record import.
9. **Billing — `/workspace/billing`.** Owner-only current plan/readiness, amount/currency, included users, actual paid-through and next billing date, invoice/payment history, supported payment-method update, approved changes, self-service cancellation and clear effective date. No invented portal or fabricated invoices. State-specific retry/refresh controls avoid duplicate subscriptions. Members receive appropriate access denial, not billing details.
10. **Recovery and public help.** `/recover` requests provider recovery with a uniform response; the existing callback/password update is completed and tested. Persistent **Need help?** links to working `/help` content and an approved functional public contact method. No passwords, keys or full card details are accepted as required support information.

Mobile forms use a single column, concise step indicator, visible labels/errors and focus, correct keyboard/autocomplete inputs, and accessible live status. Use a safe-area-aware action region that remains reachable with mobile keyboard/zoom; verify it does not obscure focused controls. Save safe onboarding fields on the server, preserve them on Back/refresh, and store no passwords, card details or verification tokens in draft storage. Test real browsers/devices, not just screenshots.

## 3. Proposed data model and state transitions

All durable application state belongs in Netlify Database. Private logos belong in Netlify Blobs with tenant metadata, validated decoding/size limits and authorized reads. This section is a schema proposal; no migration was created or applied for this signup feature.

| Record | Core data and invariants |
| --- | --- |
| Signup intent | Random opaque ID/hashed handle, allowlisted plan ID, invitation reference if applicable, verified identity binding when established, server-controlled stage, expiry and safe draft fields. No credentials/provider tokens in JSON or browser drafts. |
| Policy acceptance | Identity, policy document ID/version/content digest, server timestamp, separate Terms acceptance/Privacy acknowledgment; immutable record. Marketing consent is a distinct optional preference/change history, not bundled consent. Before verification, acceptance is pending and must be confirmed or securely bound after verification; user metadata alone is not authoritative evidence. |
| Workspace onboarding | Existing business ID, verified Owner membership, country, IANA timezone, USD reporting currency, display units, business profile, completed steps and version. Owner/business creation uses existing unique identity constraints plus transactional idempotency. |
| Plan intent/catalog mapping | Existing stable plan IDs, versioned policy/readiness, integer USD cents, included seats; separate environment-specific Square variation/location references. Price and plan mapping come only from the server. |
| Billing account/subscription | Workspace and verified billing Owner, provider environment, merchant/location/customer/subscription IDs, canonical provider status/version, cancellation schedule and reconciliation timestamps. Provider status is not authorization. |
| Checkout attempt | Workspace/actor, plan/catalog version, server quote/tax evidence, currency/amount, recurring consent version/time, durable idempotency key, provider order/payment-link/subscription references and outcome. One unresolved attempt per workspace/environment; persist before provider calls. |
| Invoice/payment coverage | Provider-unique invoice/payment/refund/dispute IDs, verified workspace/subscription association, completed amount/currency, service-period start/end, net coverage and verification timestamp. No raw card data or CVV. |
| Entitlement access | Server-derived approved plan and feature readiness, verified paid period or Free activation, seat/usage counters and access state. Never derived from a redirect, browser plan value or subscription ACTIVE alone. |
| Complimentary grant | Workspace UUID and verified Owner identity, `cottage-kitchen`, source `complimentary/platform_owner`, $0, five total users, no expiry, reason, authenticated platform-admin actor and audit. Independent from provider lifecycle. |
| Invitation | Workspace, normalized intended recipient, allowed role, hashed random single-use expiring token, issuer, consumed/revoked timestamps. Seat reservation and acceptance are transactionally serialized. No business-name grant. |
| Webhook inbox/reconciliation | Environment/event uniqueness, authenticated provider event reference, bounded payload retention, processing state, attempts, next retry, canonical provider version and sanitized diagnostic code. Durable before acknowledgement. |
| Operations/rate limits | Versioned idempotency records, bounded hashed IP/account counters, minimal signup transition events and append-only grant/billing administration audit. Platform admins see identifiers/status/errors, not customer business contents. |

Use forced RLS and composite workspace foreign keys for new tenant records. Global webhook/admin records require dedicated server-only policies, not broad customer access. Include environment in every provider uniqueness key. Unique partial indexes enforce one pending checkout/current subscription per workspace/environment; customer ID is not sufficient association. Transaction/advisory locks serialize provisioning, seats, quotas and billing transitions. A retry with the same key and different data fails. On uniqueness races, fetch the existing authorized result rather than return a partially provisioned workspace.

Keep three state dimensions: signup progress, provider billing status, and authorization/access. A single overloaded enum cannot represent an unfinished signup, a pending upgrade and an existing paid period safely.

| Visible state | Evidence and allowed transition |
| --- | --- |
| Email unverified | No activation or subscription; only safe draft and auth/help actions. |
| Setup incomplete | Verified identity, incomplete validated profile/acceptance; resume without a business-data bypass. |
| Free active | Verified setup plus approved Free policy activation; no Square subscription, card, expiry trial or future charge. |
| Checkout pending / payment processing | Exactly one unresolved provider workflow; paid access is not granted. Existing paid coverage, if any, remains separate. |
| Paid active | Canonical provider invoice and completed payment establish coverage for this exact workspace, subscription, plan, currency and service period. Partial/unsettled/uncorrelated payment does not activate access. |
| Payment failed / past due | Preserve records and paid-through access. Optional approved grace applies only to previously paid renewals, never initial failed payment. Stop unresolved future collection through supported provider controls before falling back to Free. |
| Cancelled, access through paid period | Provider cancellation confirmed; display exact end of coverage. Until confirmation show cancellation pending and reconcile, not a false success. |
| Expired | Coverage and any approved grace ended. Preserve historical reads/export and business data; approved Free/write/over-limit rules govern future actions. |
| Complimentary | Independent verified administrative grant takes precedence over trials/provider failures. Never create Square objects for this state; unfinished features stay denied. |

Every backend operation checks current identity, membership, role, access evidence and feature readiness; user-count/Free caps lock the relevant workspace. Historical reading/export also requires membership and the correct role. Receipt uploads, imports, files, exports, read endpoints and alternate action paths must not bypass these checks. Existing accounts remain unchanged until a separately approved rollout; no automatic assignment or backfill is authorized.

### Abuse, retention and operational proposal

Application limits must complement provider-enforced limits, because the managed signup/recovery endpoints can be called directly. Invite-only provider settings plus a reviewed eligibility hook protect closed beta; frontend gating is insufficient. Candidate application budgets: account creation 5/IP/hour plus 3/address/day; resend/recovery 1/address/minute, 5/address/hour and 20/IP/hour; checkout 5/workspace/hour with retries reusing the same attempt; invitations 10/workspace/hour plus 3/recipient/day. These are test-tunable operational defaults, not implemented controls or a reason to leak account existence. Store short-lived hashed keys/counters; bound bodies, provider concurrency, retries and timeouts. Do not block an authenticated retry/status query merely because a prior attempt timed out. Use origin/CSRF checks, secure cookies, and bounded callbacks, and revalidate ownership after long provider waits.

Propose expiring unclaimed signup intents after 7 days and deleting their safe drafts after 30 days; remove expired quota keys within 48 hours. Unverified provider account removal requires explicit provider/legal review and is not assumed. Billing, consent/audit retention and post-payment records require approved legal/accounting periods. Never delete paid accounts, durable workspaces or business records under abandoned-signup cleanup. Operational views show stage counts, failure codes, workspace/provider IDs and reconciliation age; no raw secrets, credentials, tokenized card sources or unrestricted record browser.

## 5. Square workflow, provider limitations and costs

Official Square documentation was fetched successfully in this audit (links below). Hosted subscription checkout supports a subscription plan variation with one paid phase, optionally preceded by one free phase. We propose one indefinite monthly paid phase and **no trial**. A quick-pay link without the recurring variation is a one-time payment and is not acceptable.

**Preferred first Sandbox experiment: Square-hosted recurring checkout.** It reduces payment-entry UI and lets Square collect card details. It does not require customers to have Square seller accounts. However, Square may find/create customers using buyer phone information; customer/email equality is not reliable workspace correlation. The checkout amount can override the plan amount if mismatched. The server must bind a unique checkout/order/attempt to a workspace and verify catalog/amount/currency/cadence before creation, then prove authoritative linkage through the resulting subscription/invoice/payment. A reusable link must not create duplicate subscriptions. The exact due-today amount, recurring terms and tax must be consistent with hosted UI. Hosted subscription checkout excludes Cash App Pay, Afterpay and application fees; this is platform revenue to the owner's seller, not an application-fee marketplace flow.

Hosted is conditional, not an irrevocable implementation decision. Sandbox must prove correlation, one unresolved subscription, safe retry, cancellation of abandoned workflows, recurring consent, tax and supported payment-method management. If these cannot be guaranteed, stop and use an integrated design review: Square Web Payments SDK tokenization, Customers API, Cards API and Subscriptions API with server idempotency and explicit recurring consent. That path offers more control over a known customer/subscription and plan management, but adds SDK initialization, tokenization/SCA/device support, security and consent responsibilities. Never store raw card data, use generic card inputs or treat one-time payment tokenization as recurring authorization. Do not promise a customer portal until the actual chosen update/management flow is verified.

Square invoices ACTIVE subscriptions even when earlier invoices were unpaid. Neither ACTIVE, a redirect, nor `charged_through_date` by itself establishes settled coverage. Square's default subscription billing is not prorated; a fixed billing anchor can produce closely spaced full-price invoices. Prefer signup-anniversary monthly billing with seller timezone/date-only fields explicitly interpreted. Plan swaps/cancellation timing and existing unpaid invoice handling must be verified against the pinned API version and Sandbox, rather than inferred from UI labels.

### Payment/webhook processing

Use separate Sandbox/Production adapters, credentials, catalog mappings and webhook notification URLs/keys. Server configuration resolves every price; reject client amounts/overrides and unknown plans. Validate current verified Owner and environment before any provider call. No provider call for Free or complimentary activation.

For webhooks, verify `x-square-hmacsha256-signature` over the exact configured notification URL plus unmodified raw body using the environment signing key, HMAC-SHA256 and constant-time comparison. Validate merchant/environment and body limits before accepting. Durable unique inbox insertion precedes acknowledgement. Process relevant supported subscription create/update, invoice payment-made/payment-failed/update, payment create/update, refund create/update and dispute events, selecting exact event names from the pinned API. Retrieve canonical provider state instead of applying arrival order. Lock subscription/workspace and retain versions, retry/backoff, dead-letter status and reconciliation attempts. Invalid signatures make no state changes.

Reconcile on delayed confirmation, ambiguous provider mutation and a periodic schedule. Verify invoice paid status and completed payment against the correct subscription, plan, currency, amount and period; account for partial payments, refunds and disputes. Scheduled reconciliation does not charge cards or create replacement subscriptions. Complimentary grants take precedence and are not removed by reconciliation. Keep provider billing status separate from authorization, and keep platform revenue out of bakery ledgers.

## 7. Implementation phases and acceptance gates

**Phase A — private account foundation, after this review.** Keep public Join the beta and provider-enforced admission. Build durable intent/acceptance/onboarding records and empty workspace provisioning with idempotency; complete verification/recovery and public help. Reuse auth, defaults and tenant controls. No production backfill or existing-record migration. Paid screens remain preview; Free activation remains gated on finalized/tested scope.

**Phase B — tested entitlement and billing foundation.** Wire server evidence/role/feature checks, approved Free limits and seat invariants; implement fake provider and state-machine tests with outbound network denial. Add Sandbox adapter and webhook/reconciliation only in isolated test contexts. Sandbox catalog setup is distinct from forbidden live plans. No real customer emails or charges.

**Phase C — Sandbox acceptance and management.** Prove initial payment/failure, delayed callback, interruption and duplicate-checkout behavior, cancellation, plan/payment-method changes and reconciliation. Prove hosted correlation/tax/consent or stop for integrated review. Real renewal tests must be labeled separately from replayed simulated events; unsupported Sandbox refund/dispute cases remain explicitly mocked.

**Phase D — complimentary access, separately confirmed.** Resolve actual workspace/verified Owner and inspect current live subscription state. After confirmation, an authorized platform admin grants `cottage-kitchen`, `complimentary/platform_owner`, $0, no card/expiry and five total users. Display **“Cottage Kitchen · Complimentary”**. No duplicate business, Square object, deferred feature activation or extra platform-admin power. Test persistence across sessions and billing/reconciliation jobs; ownership changes require explicit review. Do not invoke the prior reset or clear business data as part of this grant.

**Phase E — production release, separately approved.** Requires verified tenant isolation, working production verification/recovery mail, reviewed policies, working public support/cancellation, final entitlements and verified paid feature availability, approved recurring tax treatment, canonical domain/callbacks, passed Sandbox suite and explicit public signup/live-billing approval.
