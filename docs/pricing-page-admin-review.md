## Existing customer impact

A read-only query of the current preview database found no billing records assigned to Cottage Kitchen or Cottage Mercantile. This was a preview-branch inspection, not a Production customer audit. No member, subscription, grant or business record was edited.

The additive migration preserves **every existing billing row** with `entitlement_policy = legacy` and sets the clarified policy only as the default for future rows. Businesses without billing rows and existing complimentary/app-managed accounts retain their current behavior. The old Kitchen capability allocation is frozen in `netlify/lib/legacy-plan-scope.ts`; pricing-copy changes cannot silently alter a legacy subscription’s effective scope.

No existing account is migrated to the clarified policy. Before any future migration, report the affected businesses, active membership counts and resale/consignment holdings, then obtain explicit transition approval. Do not remove members, delete stock/history, rewrite subscriptions or move grants automatically. Production counts remain unverified and are not assumed to be zero.

## Server enforcement for the clarified policy

The new policy applies to future billing records through the database default. Existing Square payment verification, grace, cancellation and Production release gates are unchanged. Effective access continues to come from the existing server billing evaluator.

For new-policy businesses, the workspace API reads the minimal plan policy for every verified business member, including non-owner logins, without exposing billing details. Owner-only billing UI/API access remains separate. Legacy non-owner access retains the prior behavior pending a reviewed migration.

Request classification gates explicit resale and consignment actions. A second check at the transactional save boundary resolves server-stored products and linked product/recipe/batch records, catching indirect sales, receipt matching, bulk imports and forged workflow hints. Kitchen may write handmade/craft workflows but may not create resale/consignment work. Mercantile includes both modules. Reads and exports remain available, and existing refund/return/payout/reversal continuations are preserved. Denied mutations roll back in the existing workspace transaction.

The migration adds a database trigger on membership insertion/reactivation. It locks the billing row to serialize concurrent additions, counts the owner among active members and enforces Kitchen’s two-user total. Lapsed new-policy paid accounts use the existing Free fallback for further additions; current members are retained. The trigger reads billing policy under a narrowly scoped worker setting and restores that setting before continuing, including for non-owner callers. Mercantile additions beyond its initial owner are blocked with an explicit undecided-limit message until a seat allowance is approved. That block is a release prerequisite, not an invented commercial one-seat limit.

The migration changes no existing access assignment. All previously applied migrations were preserved verbatim; the new migration sorts after them and includes its generated schema snapshot.

## Readiness and limitations

Free’s proposed 5 recipes, 5 products and 10 active tracked items remain unchanged; its limits, stock coverage and standard-label subset still need launch work. Paid catalog limits remain unset, not unlimited. Existing recipe imports enforce 20/hour and 100 active drafts per business. Handmade designs enforce 1–100 material lines.

Lower paid tiers still declare basic handmade/resale workflows, while some existing Square-billed save paths require specialized upper-tier feature keys. That historical discrepancy was not used to remove or change their approved definitions. The public table marks this scope as needing access review, and the lower-tier cards avoid promising complete handmade/resale workflows. Kitchen’s handmade scope is now explicit, while its resale exclusion is enforced by the new module gate.

Automatic receipt reading remains environment-gated with no approved commercial allowance; cards do not offer it. Product URL imports require review and may fail retailer verification; barcode assistance only matches existing records. No automatic Walmart imports, unlimited use, waitlist, trial, guaranteed-profit or popularity claims were added.

Unfinished capabilities remain individually Coming soon: advanced multi-batch planning, consolidated supply requirements, period comparisons, invitations/member administration, full resale variants/returns, purchase orders, yarn stock, material lots, per-location quantities, material usage reports, dedicated profitability/activity views and sales synchronization.

Public account creation remains open; business setup remains blocked until approved Terms and Privacy are published. Paid plans cannot be activated from public signup, and Production checkout remains code-gated. No live provider request or charge was made.

**Start with a clearer picture.** For food makers building a small recipe and product catalog.

- Understand what each recipe and product costs.
- Include your time, packaging, overhead and waste.
- Keep ingredient and packaging costs together.
- Explore selling prices before choosing a price.
