## What already works versus what needs proof

The app uses `@netlify/identity` v2, with provider-managed verification/recovery/email-change capabilities. Existing login/signup SDK calls are real implementation, but earlier code lacked complete recovery/invite UX and silently ignored callback failures. Public signup is now absent; login handles existing recovery callbacks and password updates. No real mail delivery has been tested. Site protection returned 401 HTML for the provider settings probe, so registration/autoconfirm, templates, token lifetimes, sender, reply-to, SMTP transport and allowed redirects remain unverified. Do not describe these emails as operationally verified.

Support now has a separate durable outbox, protected platform settings, optional Postmark adapter and authenticated delivery-event handling; outbound delivery remains OFF. Account/invitation and optional business notification sending still need their own reviewed implementation. No application mail service account was activated. See [support tickets and setup](support-system.md). No payment provider exists. The eventual provider should own invoices, payment receipts, failed-payment and subscription notices wherever its native templates satisfy requirements. Inventory receipts are business records, not payment-provider receipts. Make an event-to-sender ownership register so a single event does not produce duplicate verification, recovery or billing emails.

## Provider recommendation and operating assumptions — not enabled

Keep Identity as the sole sender/controller of its authentication-token messages. Inspect its current transport and included allowance before adding SMTP. A custom authenticated mail domain may need DNS and provider SMTP configuration if the default transport cannot meet delivery/branding requirements; verify compatibility, limits and cost first.

Use the eventual billing provider's native customer notices. Stripe Billing plus hosted Checkout/Customer Portal is the recommended candidate for workspace subscriptions, subject to owner review of country, tax responsibility, fees and payout eligibility. It is not installed or activated. Webhooks must update server-owned workspace subscriptions; browser success pages do not grant entitlements. Do not create duplicate application invoices or receipts by default.

If invitations and security notices need application delivery beyond Identity, evaluate a transactional service such as Postmark for message streams, authenticated domains and bounce/suppression handling. Choose a provider only after monthly volume, region, retention and budget approval. No current quote or account-specific limit is verified. A planning example at 100 new identities and 20 Cottage Kitchen workspaces with four invites each produces roughly 200 verification/recovery messages (assuming two per identity), 80 initial invites plus controlled resends, and up to one billing notice per paying workspace/event; actual failed payments and security events vary. This is a sizing assumption, not a promise of included sends or price.

At this audit stage, incremental provider spend is **$0 activated**. Existing account subscriptions may already incur charges; they were not inspected or cancelled. Confirm official current pricing, included volume, overage, dedicated-domain requirements and attachment limits in the selected accounts. Budget hosting/function/database/blob usage separately. Do not promise a free tier or fixed mail price without a current quote. Optional digests scale with opted-in recipients and frequency, not page views.

## Public price/limit references checked during this audit

The public [Postmark pricing page](https://postmarkapp.com/pricing) listed a Developer allowance of **100 emails/month, no overages**, and Basic starting at **$15/month for 10,000 emails**, with extra messages at **$1.80/1,000**. These are public list prices, not an account quote or a selected/activated plan. Sender approval, domain verification, account restrictions and the actual contract still require owner review. Do not buy a 10,000-message plan merely to test, and do not send real mail on the free allowance without approval. Identity's current included mail quota and SMTP requirements remain unverified; its existing account mail may avoid another application service until invitations/security notices are implemented.

The public [Stripe Billing pricing page](https://stripe.com/billing/pricing) listed pay-as-you-go Billing at **0.7% of Billing volume**, with the displayed US card example **2.9% + $0.30 per successful charge** separately. At those illustrative domestic-card rates, a $10/$25/$40 charge costs approximately $0.66/$1.20/$1.74 before other fees, taxes, disputes, refunds, international cards or currency conversion. Verify the owner's jurisdiction and actual account contract. This is an operating-cost illustration, not an approved payment implementation. No custom portal domain add-on or annual commitment is proposed.

## Required setup before any send test

**Owner/account side:** approve support and billing contacts; configure `mycottagepantry.com` in Netlify/DNS/TLS and Identity site/redirect settings; approve templates and sender/reply addresses; authenticate mail domain with SPF/DKIM and reviewed DMARC; inspect native Identity and future billing message ownership/limits; approve a specific recipient and real-email test. No unverified support email was invented on the website.

**Code side:** durable workspace-scoped message outbox and provider event IDs; least-privilege recipient selection; idempotency/deduplication; bounded retry/backoff for transient failures only; handle permanent failures/bounces/suppressed recipients without repeated attempts; signed delivery webhooks; sanitized operational logging and security audit. Keep raw recovery/invitation tokens out of logs, errors, analytics, query strings and persisted debugging. Store only token hashes for app-owned invites. Provider credentials stay server-side.

Resend/recovery needs per-account and per-IP rate limits with uniform responses, cooldowns and bounded daily sends. Avoid account enumeration. Correct canonical production URLs must be generated from trusted server configuration; never trust request Host/client URL for sensitive email links. Verification/reset/invite flows need expiry, already-used and invalid-token tests on the custom domain. Existing provider defaults are not a substitute for verification.

Default application outbound mail OFF in previews/tests and allow only a dedicated sink/allowlisted test address in explicit test mode. Test Identity's preview callback and sending behavior independently: a preview copy of a database does not isolate provider delivery. CI uses fake transports, never real recipients. Restrict production sending to approved context/domain; do not copy production mail credentials into previews. Review bounce hooks and suppression behavior before beta.

## Optional business notifications — proposed only

An opted-in low-stock digest uses an agreed threshold crossing or unresolved-state digest, never a message on every page load. Record workspace + rule + item + threshold episode + recipient + delivery window as a durable uniqueness key. Include a cooldown and daily/weekly frequency ceiling. A restock resets the episode only after verified state change.

Production reminders use the workspace's IANA timezone, handle daylight-saving transitions, store a deterministic scheduled instant and deduplicate workspace/batch/recipient/reminder offset. Rescheduling/cancellation invalidates old jobs. No timezone is inferred from a browser when not configured.

At dispatch, recheck active membership, the relevant view permission, feature entitlement and recipient preference. Removed users receive nothing; a job cannot carry stale permission grants. Use org-scoped queries and cache keys, and include only data that recipient can read. Preferences and unsubscribe controls apply to optional mail separately from essential security/account/subscription notices. Sending failures never change stock, cost or accounting records. No such notifications are advertised until these behaviors and delivery are implemented and tested.
