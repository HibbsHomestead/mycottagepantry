# Support helper and internal access

The existing Help control opens the AI support helper inside the shared popup. It answers advisory questions using the same reviewed articles displayed on `/help`. The articles cover login, inventory and conversions, product/barcode imports, production and kits, labels, billing, and mobile troubleshooting. They were checked against the existing implementations in `verified-session`, Add Item and product import, inventory receiving, production, labels, billing entitlement/manual access, and the shared dialog. Update `src/lib/support-knowledge.ts` when these workflows change; retrieval and the public guide use that one source.

The helper uses the existing OpenAI SDK with Netlify AI Gateway and `gpt-4.1-mini`. Credentials remain in the server runtime. No new third-party credential was required, and a real app-use question passed through the configured provider. This does not enable the separate product-research assistant or live billing.

The requester must opt in before sending a message to the provider. Only redacted user text, a bounded recent conversation, and relevant help articles are sent. The helper retrieves current guides per question and follow-up. With separate status-sharing consent, its allowlisted tools verify current identity/membership, recorded business billing and integration status where the business role permits it, receipt attempt statuses for permitted finance roles, and the requester’s unresolved label recovery count. Every tool call revalidates identity, role, business membership and conversation audience. Inventory contents, payment details, credentials and general business mutations remain unavailable. Unsupported questions and obvious instruction-injection requests receive an uncertainty response. Provider failures preserve the conversation and offer guides or support. Retrieved links come from fixed article IDs, and all messages render as plain text.

Diagnostics require a separate explicit attachment and show a sanitized preview. The existing error message and stack allowlists rebuild the attachment on both client and server; arbitrary fields, raw error text, credentials, item contents, and payment details are omitted. Diagnostics include the public app version, safe page, time, and available allowlisted error information. They remain outside every AI provider request.

Contact support requires a reviewed issue summary and attaches the retained conversation to the existing private ticket. The admin inbox is linked from Administration and is available on the admin host. The admin host restores and validates the separate host-only platform portal session and current enabled database grant; it does not require a customer Identity cookie or duplicate Identity role. Customer Identity sessions cannot substitute for the portal session. Signed-out, forbidden and temporary loading failures have separate states. Ticket permissions and forced database policies protect replies and private notes. Un-escalated chats stay private to their requester. Conversations are scoped to verified business membership, including accounts with multiple ovens. An AI suggestion sends no reply; the administrator must choose a draft, review/edit it, and use the normal reply control. Private notes, screenshots and diagnostic attachments are excluded from suggestions. Ordinary tickets require separate requester consent for staff AI review; reviewed helper escalations explain that use.

The default limits are 20 attempts per person, 60 per oven and 400 platform-wide in a rolling 24 hours, with a ten-second cooldown, 30 messages per conversation, at most three provider requests of 550 output tokens each and four allowlisted tool calls per question, a 20-second per-request provider timeout, and no automatic retries. Failed provider attempts count. Verified platform administrators can pause replies and change bounded quotas, cooldown and retention in the inbox. Thirty-day usage visibility includes attempts, failures and provider-reported tokens; it includes a clearly labeled reference token-cost estimate, not an invoice, and excludes message content. Interrupted provider requests may have incomplete token totals; actual Gateway credits are reviewed in Netlify AI usage. Usage-policy changes require explicit review confirmation.

Chats and optional diagnostics expire 30 days after creation by default; new-chat retention is configurable from one to 90 days. Expired attachments are immediately unavailable through the API, and a daily scheduled cleanup deletes expired chats and usage metadata older than 30 days. A requester can delete a conversation sooner, including its ticket attachment. Existing support ticket summaries and replies remain. Provider requests set `store: false`; OpenAI abuse-monitoring retention can still apply for up to 30 days. Neither zero data retention nor deletion of provider logs is claimed. No business data is stored in browser storage.

The restricted inbox also contains a separate private administrator assistant. Its conversations use an explicit admin audience and remain visible only to their verified author through the admin session; they cannot be attached to customer tickets. The administrator selects an inbox thread to scope account/business tools to its requester and business. The tools can retrieve support ticket numbers/statuses, current account verification, recorded billing/integration metadata, and sanitized diagnostic counts. Private notes, receipt contents, raw errors, secrets and payment references are excluded from model inputs. Billing reconciliation reuses the existing provider recovery implementation. The model can only issue a server-stored proposal; a separate human confirmation endpoint requires fresh existing MFA, checks ticket/business scope again, prevents replay, records the outcome, and verifies the recorded reconciliation timestamp before reporting success. Uncertain outcomes remain unverified and are not automatically retried.

The reviewed knowledge covers the implemented Item Type restriction, unsaved Add Item drafts, ingredient/craft material separation, linked food recipes and historical stock/cost protection; recipe categories and base recipes; counts, receipts, products/handmade designs/kits; label recovery; billing and support privacy. Existing saved Item Type is fixed even without stock. The supported alternative is Inventory → Add Item → Item Type → Craft Material, retaining the original ingredient and its recipe/ledger/history references. There is no in-place conversion or automatic stock/history transfer. Knowledge links use implemented navigation, including `/oven/billing`. Update this shared knowledge when workflows change.

Cottage Mercantile is approved for internal assignment with six total users including the owner. The existing admin preview/confirmation, immutable grant history, suspension precedence and override expiry/revocation rules remain authoritative. Public signup eligibility and live Stripe eligibility are separate from internal assignment. Mercantile remains blocked for live Stripe purchase; other existing commercial gates remain in place. The tier selector uses singular “1 user.”

The shared purple Back to top control follows the page or topmost popup's actual scrolling element. It appears after roughly 90% of that viewport has been scrolled, hides background controls during popups, respects reduced motion, and remains focusable through a keyboard-initiated return to the top. The popup dock sits above footer actions without changing popup anchoring, and the arrow adjusts around visible interactive controls. Shared dialogs retain caller-provided refs alongside their internal scroll scope. Inventory Table retains real columns on smaller screens, bounds/wraps Item names beside their thumbnail, removes the duplicate category, aligns numbers, and uses modest padding. Cards and business calculations are unchanged.
