# Label editor and template gallery layout

The editor sidebar previously used 180–230px desktop tracks while its asset buttons inherited `white-space: nowrap` and its grid children retained their intrinsic minimum widths. Layer names were deliberately truncated. The gallery's final override created 220px cards and capped previews at 200px, cutting off the vertically stacked front/back preview. Shared text-button margins added substantial gaps and consumed the cards' limited width.

The sidebar now uses 280–320px desktop tracks. Its grids and text children can shrink and wrap while icons, thumbnails, and layer controls retain their size. The sidebar scrolls vertically with reserved scrollbar space, and the center canvas track can shrink independently. The right settings panel stays beside the canvas at the checked desktop widths; at smaller widths it moves below the canvas. No horizontal overflow hiding was added.

The gallery now uses flexible columns with a 320px preferred minimum, filling unused columns when only a few templates exist. Cards have a consistent 230px preview area, followed by the name, type/revision information, primary actions, and a compact secondary action group. Open template and Assign to products remain visible. Secondary actions fit without requiring a menu. Single labels retain their aspect ratio, and paired labels appear side by side with separate Front/Back size captions outside their artwork.

Chromium checks passed at **1762 × 854** (the supplied image size) and **1280 × 854**. The real application rendered three synthetic templates matching the screenshot's names, dimensions, and formats: Front/Back Template (3 × 3in per side), Food Label (3 × 4in), and BreadLabelTemplate (3 × 4in). Private saved template data was not accessed. All three cards fit across the wider gallery; the narrower gallery used two columns. Cards measured about 471–475px wide and 445px high.

The checks examined actual text rectangles and scroll widths, so clipping or hiding overflowing text could not make them pass. All action labels and asset names fit; both preview sides stayed inside the preview area. A long asset name wrapped alongside an intact 38px thumbnail. A long layer name wrapped beside visible lock and visibility controls. Elements and Assets scrolled internally, the final asset was reachable, and the sidebar had no horizontal scrolling. The center canvas remained at least 300px wide, with a usable right settings panel. Screenshots were also inspected visually.

ESLint reported the same 29 errors and seven warnings in the existing editor as the unchanged baseline; neither changed test file had lint findings. Label layouts, dimensions, binding logic, printing/export logic, saved data, front/back relationships, assignments, and action handlers were unchanged. No build command or manual deployment was run.
