# Cottage Pantry label artwork

All fourteen SVG illustrations and their prepared PNG derivatives are original artwork created for Cottage Pantry. No stock icons, traced artwork, commercial branding, or third-party images were used. No paid generation service was used.

These assets are dedicated under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Commercial use, modification, and distribution within the app are permitted without attribution.

The owner approved the bananas, spiced orange, cinnamon roll, and cranberry orange samples as the visual style for this collection. Every image uses a 120 × 96 view box, a 2.2-unit black outline, white interiors, and transparent exterior space. Each source is restricted to static paths, circles, and ellipses with no scripts, event handlers, fonts, external references, embedded rasters, filters, or gradients.

SVGs are used for previews and browser printing. The 1200 × 960 transparent PNG derivatives are prepared once from the same sources for PDF/PNG export; they provide at least 300 DPI up to 4 × 3.2 inches. Larger placements receive the existing resolution warning. PDF image export remains raster-based; it does not claim vector PDF output.
